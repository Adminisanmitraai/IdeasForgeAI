13 / THE MEETING EXPERIENCE

The best seat is for a conversation.

4tables

Two tables for each province, with equal priority and shared hospitality.

Upholstered chairs, small round tables, usable power access and a quiet ambience enable meaningful discussions despite the shallow 4 m footprint.

01

Comfort

Support 30–45 minute conversations without fatigue.

02

Privacy

Use modest acoustic slats and partial screening, never bulky enclosed rooms.

03

Flexibility

Allow table appointments and walk-in introductions to coexist.

✓Power points at information and meeting positions

✓Soft lighting on delegates, not only on exhibits

For only 4 m booth depth, the layout needs strict coordination of furniture dimensions, inward seating pull-back, and accessible approach.

17 / CLIENT & ORGANIZER APPROVALS

Decisions before detailed design.

Confirm actual access, support structures, and whether the 9 m boundary can be opened.

02Technical rules

Confirm IEW 2027 maximum height, setback, hanging-sign, floor and services regulations.

03Provincial content

04Meeting capacity

Validate delegate count, daily schedule, hospitality level and scope/budget priorities.

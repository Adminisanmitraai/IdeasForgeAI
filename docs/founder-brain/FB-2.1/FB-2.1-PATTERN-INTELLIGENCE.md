## Safety boundary
Pattern intelligence is descriptive, not autonomous judgment. Ambiguous outcomes are not guessed as success/failure. Every reported pattern carries source IDs back to cognitive memory. No execution, filesystem, subprocess, network, or provider capability is introduced.

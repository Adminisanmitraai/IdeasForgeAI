FOS-BE-5 — Platform Event Model

Baseline: 75% certified at FOS-BE-4.
Target after certification: 79% (+4%).
Progress remains 75% until all acceptance gates pass.

Objective
Introduce a versioned internal Founder OS event envelope and adapt existing Terminal polling and chat streaming into it without breaking existing transports.

Core requirements
- Stable event_id, event_type, source, timestamp, correlation_id and causation_id.
- Versioned contract with deterministic serialization.
- Bounded payload/metadata suitable for activity-feed projection.
- Terminal session-event adapter preserving execution/session sequence.
- Chat stream adapter preserving incremental delta order.
- Activity-feed projection independent of transport implementation.

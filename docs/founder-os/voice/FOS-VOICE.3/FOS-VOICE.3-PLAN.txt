FOS-VOICE.3 — Voice Permission/Consent + Cost Metering + Audit Events

Baseline: 83% certified at FOS-VOICE.2.
Target after certification: 85% (+2%).
Progress remains 83% until every acceptance gate passes.

Objective
Enforce voice permissions before ForgeVoice execution, add duration-aware usage/cost metering, and emit auditable high-risk voice events without exposing raw reference assets or provider credentials.

Scope
- Runtime product-scope and restricted-use checks.
- Authorized cloning consent validation and explicit approval gating.
- STT/TTS duration, character, cache and local-compute cost estimation.
- Per-project/product/agent/voice/language/session cost records.
- Safe audit events for permission decisions and governed completion.
- Normalize ForgeVoice usage payloads into VoiceUsage.

Out of scope
- Voice DNA database persistence.
- Real provider billing APIs.
- ForgeCall production migration.
- STT/TTS model deployment.

FOS-VOICE.2 — ForgeVoice Gateway Client + Routing Decision Engine

Baseline: 81% certified at FOS-VOICE.1.
Target after certification: 83% (+2%).
Progress remains 81% until all gates pass.

Objective
Connect Founder OS orchestration to ForgeVoice through a transport-neutral service client and deterministic routing engine, while keeping all STT/TTS/cloning execution outside Founder OS.

Scope
- local-first routing for DRAFT/STANDARD/REALTIME where eligible;
- ForgeVoice external/premium preference for PREMIUM/CINEMATIC;
- language, cost, latency and availability filtering;
- ordered fallback chain and bounded retry intent;
- transport-neutral ForgeVoice client with no provider SDK dependency;
- correlation-aware platform events for route selection, provider failure and completion;
- normalized ForgeVoice unavailable behavior.

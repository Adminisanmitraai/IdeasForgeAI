Responsible AI

Useful AI with people in control.

IdeasForge AI is building applied AI systems intended to assist people and organizations without hiding consequential automation. Our current responsible-AI direction emphasizes human oversight, understandable behavior, bounded access and proportional controls.

Human oversight

AI recommendations and consequential actions are intended to remain subject to appropriate user or administrator control.

Design principle

Transparent behavior

Generated content, recommendations and executed actions should remain distinguishable so users can understand the role AI played.

Design principle

Permission boundaries

AI capabilities are intended to operate within the permissions and context required for the requested workflow rather than undisclosed broad access.

Design principle

Proportional automation

Controls should become stronger as a workflow becomes more consequential, particularly where actions affect devices, employees, external platforms or financial commitments.

Design principle

Reviewable outcomes

Important workflows are being developed toward clearer action history, approval context and verification so outcomes can be reviewed.

In development

Continuous improvement

Governance, security and product safeguards are being refined as the product family moves through development and controlled pilot readiness.

Current stage
Current-stage statement

This statement describes IdeasForge AI's product-development principles. It is not a claim of certification, regulatory approval, external endorsement, or universal implementation across every product and integration. Product-specific safeguards will continue to be documented as capabilities mature.

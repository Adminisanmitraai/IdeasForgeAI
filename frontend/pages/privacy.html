Privacy

Privacy with clear purpose and control.

IdeasForge AI is developing its products around purposeful data access, permission boundaries and user or organizational control. This page describes the current privacy direction; product-specific practices may differ as implementations and integrations mature.

Purpose-limited access

Products are intended to request information that is relevant to the capability or workflow being used rather than relying on undisclosed broad access.

Design principle

Permissions

Connected services and device capabilities are intended to operate through explicit permissions and controls appropriate to each integration.

Implementation varies

Data minimization

Where implemented, products are intended to collect and retain only information required for their disclosed purpose. Retention behavior may vary by product and provider.

Implementation varies

User and organization control

IdeasForge AI is designing connected access so permissions can be managed or revoked where supported by the relevant product or external provider.

In development

Third-party services

Some products may integrate external AI, cloud, communication or platform services. Those services may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Product dependent

Security and accountability

Security controls, auditability and privacy documentation are being strengthened as the product family moves toward broader organizational pilots.

Current stage
Current-stage notice

This overview is public product-direction documentation, not a claim of certification or universal implementation across every IdeasForge AI product. More detailed product-specific privacy notices will be published as deployment models and data flows are finalized. Privacy questions can be sent to hello@ideasforgeai.com.

Responsible AI foundation

Trust by design.

Users should be able to understand what the AI is doing, what it can access and when human approval is required.

Permission boundariesAccess is scoped to capabilities required for the requested task.
Human controlConsequential actions are designed to remain under appropriate user or administrator control.
Data minimizationWhere implemented, products request and retain only information required for their disclosed purpose.
AuditabilityImportant operations are designed to preserve clear action history and verification.
Transparent AIRecommendations, generated content and automated actions are kept distinguishable.
Revocable accessConnected services and device permissions are designed to remain manageable by users or organizations.

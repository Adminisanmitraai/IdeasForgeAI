FABRICATION GATES / BEFORE TECHNICAL SIGN-OFF

What must be verified before approval.

01 / GEOMETRY

Organizer CAD

Confirm true plot orientation, access sides, perimeter rules, height and any setbacks.

02 / EQUIPMENT

Product fixing

Verify actual dispenser weight, anchorage, electrical and protection requirements.

03 / HOSPITALITY

Room services

Confirm meeting occupancy, furniture sizes, locker door clearance and coffee service.

04 / AV + POWER

Electrical design

Confirm load schedule, screen dimensions, floor cabling, circuit segregation and safety.

DESIGN-GATE PRINCIPLE
Do not approve fabrication from a concept render alone. Produce measured CAD, elevations, structural methods and service drawings after concept selection.

RECOMMENDATION / SHORTLIST

A disciplined way to make the decision.

OUR DESIGN RECOMMENDATION

Shortlist two strong directions.

Take Signature Ribbon forward for a premium, memorable corporate identity and retain Precision Showcase as the cleanest product-led alternate.

Use Energy Edge if the management priority is a more expressive, technology-first architectural statement.

A final recommendation must follow a dimension-accurate 4 × 8 m CAD feasibility review and preliminary BOQ, including enclosed-room clearances.
